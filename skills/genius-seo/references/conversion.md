# Turning Readers into Clients

Rankings and citations are only useful if readers take the next step. Use this for every post and every content plan.

## The equation
**Clients = readers × lead rate × close rate.**
SEO and AEO raise readers. This file raises the lead rate. The offer and the sales call raise the close rate. Measure all three; never guess them.

## 1. Pick topics with buying pain
- Score business potential 0–3 (`seo-checklist.md`). Prefer **2–3**: the reader's problem is exactly what the product or service solves.
- Use the **customer's own words** as topics ("no callbacks in 3 months", not "job search optimization").
- Map every post to **one** product or service page before writing.

## 2. Earn trust before asking
- **Answer first**, then the detail. People scan: put the key point in the first words of headings, paragraphs and bullets ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/)).
- **Be specific and original:** scripts, timelines, worked examples and checklists the top results don't have. Google rewards "non-commodity" content.
- **Be accurate:** every fact sourced and dated. In regulated topics, one wrong fact loses the reader.
- **Show who's behind it:** a real author with a role, and real experience ("in our work with 300+ clients…" only if true).
- **No hype, no guarantees.** Honesty converts better than promises, and protects the brand.

## 3. A ladder of CTAs, one main ask
| Placement | CTA type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| In the body, where the problem comes up | **Contextual text link** to the matching service | "…that's what our [Resume Overhaul](/services/resume) rebuilds." |
| Mid-post, for readers not ready to talk | **Soft offer:** a free checklist, template or tool | "Run through our free [Resume Checklist](/resources) first." |
| End of post | **One primary CTA**, the main conversion | "**Book a Free Strategy Call**" → booking page |

Rules:
- **One primary ask per page.** Several competing asks dilute attention.
- **Name the outcome, not the action:** "Find out what's blocking your interviews" beats "Submit".
- **Remove friction:** say what happens next, how long it takes and what it costs ("Free · 30 minutes · No pitch").
- **Match the CTA to intent:** an informational reader gets a soft offer plus the main CTA; a commercial reader goes straight to the main CTA.
- **Keep CTAs in visible HTML:** not only in images, not hidden in tabs (Microsoft: hidden content may not be read).

## 4. Capture readers who aren't ready yet
- Free resources (checklists, playbooks) in exchange for an email, followed by a short, useful email sequence that ends in the main CTA.
- Retarget with distribution: post each article on LinkedIn, Reddit or Quora and in newsletters the same week.

## 5. Measure what converts
| Track | How |
|---|---|
| CTA clicks per post | GA4 event (e.g. `book_call_click`) with the post slug |
| Leads per post | Form event (e.g. `generate_lead`) with the source page |
| AI-assisted visits | GA4 channel for chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com |
| AI citations | Search Console Generative AI performance report; Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance |
| Real source | "How did you hear about us?" on the form or the call. AI-influenced visits often look "direct" in analytics |

Semrush found AI-search visitors convert at **4.4×** the rate of organic visitors, so small AI traffic can matter. Check this against your own data.

**Monthly review:** rank posts by leads, not traffic. Refresh the ones with traffic but no leads (better CTA, closer service link) and the ones with leads but falling traffic (update facts and dates).

## Pre-publish conversion checklist
- [ ] The topic is a real buying pain (business potential 2–3)
- [ ] It links to exactly one primary service in the body, where it fits
- [ ] There's one soft offer mid-post (resource or tool)
- [ ] There's one primary CTA at the end, stating the outcome and removing friction
- [ ] The brand is mentioned once, in the CTA
- [ ] No unsourced claims, guarantees or hype
- [ ] Tracking events fire on CTA clicks and form submits
